About Me

An Electrical, Electronic & Telecommunication Engineering graduate with over a decade of professional experience in the research, design, and development of embedded systems. He is deeply involved in firmware development using C/C++ and advanced schematic and multilayer PCB design utilizing tools like Altium Designer and KiCAD. His career is defined by a commitment to research and development, frequently updating technology through the creation of IoT-enabled platforms and sophisticated industrial equipment, such as HMI-driven machine controllers and automated power management systems. As a result of managing the entire product lifecycle—from initial concept and prototyping to mass manufacturing—he has established a solid foundation in the research, design, validation, troubleshooting, and servicing of complex electronic systems.

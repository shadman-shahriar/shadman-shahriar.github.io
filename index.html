Hello! I'm Shadman Shahriar, an Electronics and Embedded Systems Engineer with a passion for bringing innovative ideas to life through hardware and software integration. My journey in electronics began with a Diploma in Electronics Engineering, followed by a Bachelor of Science in EETE, providing me with a robust theoretical and practical foundation.

A passionate Embedded Software Engineer 🚀 with experience in Microcontrollers, Sensors, firmware, and Embedded Systems. Good working knowledge of C/C++, PCB, and circuit design with manufacturing and quality assurance.
